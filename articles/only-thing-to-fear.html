The only thing to fear is...

No one believes her. But what about the blood in the pool? And the horrible injuries to her leg?

✍ Nicola Prentis·📅 Story·⏱ 5 min read·📊 B1 Easy

No one believed me about what happened in the swimming pool, of course. I don't blamesay someone is responsible for something bad them. I wouldn't believe it if any of them said it either. I liked showing how much I know, but everyone is missing the pointnot understanding the most important thing. The point is how did the shark end up in the pool? They didn't ask me that.

You'd think they'd want to know how I made that shark appear out of nowherevery suddenly and unexpectedly. It's the feara strong feeling of being afraid that did it, I'm sure. My teacher says the only thing to fear is fear itself. I always thought that meant "Don't be afraid." But now I know the truth. "Fear makes bad things happen."
