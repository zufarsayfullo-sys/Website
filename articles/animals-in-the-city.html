Animals in the city

Wild animals in cities can cause lots of problems. From baboons to squirrels, you'll learn a lot about urban wildlife.

✍ Robin Newton·📅 Article·⏱ 6 min read·📊 B1 Easy

Recently, there have been many reports about big animals coming into towns and cities. There have been bears in Vancouver parks, leopardslarge wild cats with spotted fur on the streets of Mumbai and wild pigspigs that live in nature, not on farms in gardens in Berlin. Is it a good thing or is it dangerous for us and the animals?

Wild animals usually come into cities to look for food. In Cape Town, South Africa, baboonslarge monkeys with long faces sometimes come into the suburbsareas just outside a city where people live. They eat fruit from gardens and go into people's kitchens and take food from cupboards and fridges! Baboons are strong animals and sometimes they scarefrighten someone children and fight with pet dogs.

But the city can be dangerous for baboons too. Sometimes, baboons are hurtinjured or in pain in car accidents and the sugar in human food can be very bad for their teeth. The city councila group of people elected to govern a city in Cape Town has a team of Baboon Monitorspeople who watch and check something. Their job is to find baboons in the city and take them back to the countryside.

In Berlin, groups of wild pigs sometimes come into the city. Pigs eat flowers and plants and dig in gardens and parks. Some city residents like the pigs and give them food. But the city council is worried about the traffic accidents. They have told people to stop giving the pigs food and have put up fencesstructures of wood or wire that keep things in or out.

In Moscow in Russia, there are 35,000 wild dogsdogs that live without owners. They live in parks, empty houses, markets and train stations. Some live alone and others live in packsgroups of animals that hunt or live together (a pack is the name for a group of dogs).

In 2010, scientists studied the dogs and found very interesting facts: Packs have leaderspeople or animals that lead the others — the most intelligentable to learn and understand quickly dogs, not the biggest. Dogs know it's safer to cross the street with people and some dogs understand traffic lightslights that tell cars when to stop and go.

Dogs have learnt that people give more food to small, cutepretty and attractive, often small dogs than to big ones. The cutest dogs in a pack wait on the street for people to give them food. When they have got some food, they sharegive part of something to others it with the other dogs. Some dogs have started travelling on the Moscow underground trainstrains that run in tunnels under a city.

A lot of people in Moscow like the dogs. They give them food and water. The winter in Moscow is very cold with temperatures of −10°C. It can be hard for dogs to survivestay alive in a difficult situation but some city residents have built small hutssmall simple houses or shelters for the dogs to live in during the winter.

For many big animals, cities are dangerous places and they need our help to return to the countryside.
